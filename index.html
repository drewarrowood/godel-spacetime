Gödel spacetime (1949)

Rotating-dust universe · illustrative causal structure

  • Blue cones: r < rcrit, ordinary causal
  • Orange cones: r ≥ rcrit, φ can be timelike
  • Pink ring: closed timelike curve
  • Green helix: ordinary near-axis worldline
  • Tan helices: co-rotating dust
  • Gold ring: critical radius ≈ 2
